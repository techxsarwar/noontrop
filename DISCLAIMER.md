## 2. Radio Frequency & Telecommunications Regulations

NoonTrop operates using standard built-in device Wi-Fi hardware adapters conforming to IEEE 802.11 standards and operating within standard unlicensed ISM frequency bands (2.4 GHz and 5 GHz).

1. **Standard Wi-Fi Protocols**: NoonTrop uses standard operating system APIs (`WifiP2pManager` on Android, `MultipeerConnectivity` on iOS) to manage peer connections and adheres to standard power transmission levels programmed into the host device hardware.
2. **Local Wireless Laws**: Users are solely responsible for ensuring that their use of Wi-Fi Direct and peer-to-peer radio frequency broadcasting complies with all applicable local, national, and international telecommunications laws and spectrum management authorities (such as the FCC in the United States, ETSI/CE in the European Union, TRAI in India, or equivalent national bodies).

NoonTrop is **NOT** a certified emergency telecommunication service:
- It **cannot** dial emergency numbers (911, 112, 999, 100, 000).

- Do not rely on NoonTrop as your sole method of communication in life-threatening scenarios where certified emergency services or satellite transceivers are required.
